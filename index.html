/ 15 — THREE RULES

Three rules to live by.

If you forget everything else, remember these. They will keep every outfit on-palette without thinking.

i.

Always anchor near the face.

The color closest to your jaw does the most work. If your suit is off-palette, put a Deep Winter color into the shirt, tie, or scarf nearest your face. Burgundy ties save warm-grey suits.

ii.

Depth, not brightness.

Your palette is high-contrast but not Bright Winter. Sapphire over electric blue. Ruby over fire-engine red. Saturated jewels over neons. The Adam Driver–Keanu Reeves register, never Spring-clear.

iii.

Replace, don't just add.

The biggest ROI on this analysis is swapping: tan shoes → oxblood; cream shirt → optic white; camel coat → charcoal; warm chocolate belt → black; yellow gold watch → silver; medium-wash jeans → dark indigo. One swap per week for ten weeks.
